# Majora's Mask (2 Ship 2 Harkinian)

Unofficial fan project. It is not made, supported or endorsed by the 2 Ship 2 Harkinian team, the Harbour Masters or
the Archipelago project.

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and
export a config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask is played through the 2 Ship 2 Harkinian PC port, with the options of that port's
own randomizer. Items are shuffled between the locations of this game and the other games of the multiworld. The
logic is generated from 2 Ship 2 Harkinian's own logic, so a seed is beatable under the same rules as its built-in
randomizer.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Majora. With Triforce Hunt, find the required number of Triforce Pieces instead.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

Always: chests, NPC rewards, heart pieces, stray fairies, minigames and songs. By option: owl statues, shops, Tingle
maps, boss remains, cows, enemy drops, frogs, butterflies, freestanding items, wonder items, Gold Skulltulas, pots,
crates, barrels, snowballs, grass, trees and beehives.

Options can also add items that have no location of their own: boss souls, enemy souls, NPC souls, the ability to
swim, ocarina buttons, the six half-days of the three-day cycle (Clock Shuffle), extra songs, the Tycoon's Wallet,
the Skeleton Key, traps and Triforce Pieces.

With Shuffle Dungeon Entrances, the doors of the four temples lead into a random one of them. A temple's exit, and
the blue warp after its boss, lead back out of the door you came in through.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

Every shuffled item. Small keys, boss keys and stray fairies can be limited to their own dungeon or be given at the
start.

## What does another world's item look like in Majora's Mask?

It shows the Archipelago logo: gray for filler, in full color for useful items, and in full color with a small arrow
below it for progression items and traps. Hints, shops and the "You found ..." text name the item and its owner.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

The item is given right away and a notification names the item and who sent it.

## What is needed to play?

The client and the apworld from the same release of
[Updated 2S2H Archipelago](https://github.com/junkx420/Updated-2S2H-Archipelago), and your own legally obtained copy
of Majora's Mask. See the setup guide.
